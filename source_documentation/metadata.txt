DTMB 5415 Hull Dataset – Matrix Structure Description
============================================

General
-------
Total samples: 9001
Column-wise organization: each column corresponds to one configuration
Column 1: baseline (DTMB 5415 reference hull)
Columns 2–9001: sampled configurations

All matrices share the same number of columns (9001)

---

SCALING INFORMATION
-------------------

Geometry:
- Full scale representation
- Lpp = 142 m

Seakeeping quantities:
- Computed at full scale

Calm-water quantities:
- Computed at model scale
- Lpp = 5.720 m

Reference model-scale parameters:
- Displacement: 0.549 tonnes
- Beam: 0.760 m
- Draft: 0.248 m
- LCG: 2.884 m
- VCG: 0.056 m
- Water density: 998.5 kg/m³
- Kinematic viscosity: 1.09E-06 m²/s
- Gravity: 9.803 m/s²

---

GEOMETRY
--------

xMatrix: (2250 × 9001)
yMatrix: (2250 × 9001)
zMatrix: (2250 × 9001)

Description:
Flattened hull surface coordinates (full scale).

Total geometric DOFs:
3 × 2250 = 6750

---

DESIGN VARIABLES
----------------

variablesMatrix: (27 × 9001)

Description:
Normalized design variables in [-1,1].

---

LUMPED PHYSICAL QUANTITIES
--------------------------

coefStatMatrix: (3 × 9001)
    Wave resistance coefficient (Cw) – model scale

accelerationRMSMatrix: (3 × 9001)
    RMS vertical acceleration (az) – full scale

motionRMSMatrix: (3 × 9001)
    RMS pitch motion (xi5) – full scale

Rows correspond to:

Row 1 → Fr = 0.25
Row 2 → Fr = 0.33
Row 3 → Fr = 0.41
